The double diamond, with AI

Working out what to ask and getting it made are different jobs — they need different instructions, and a different way of working.

Before the plan: think with it

What you tell it
Open asks, few rules: “give me five ways to…”, “what would you need to know?”, “what am I assuming?” Ask for options, and for questions back.
How you work with it
Many short turns, one chat. React to options instead of specifying up front — your reaction is the context it was missing. End by writing the plan down.

After the plan: build with it

What you tell it
The plan, as written: one task, the context it needs, the labels defined, the constraints, one example of the shape. Nothing extra.
How you work with it
New chat, one ask — three drafts if you like. Judge them against the plan, not the chat; fix the plan, run again. Same plan, same shape: that is what a skill saves.
The plan is the hinge: the first diamond writes it, the second runs on it. Skip the first and you build a guess. Never leave it and nothing ships.
